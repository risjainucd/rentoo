Privacy at Rentoo.

What we collect, what we don't, and how to ask us to delete it. Written in plain English by Rishabh, not by a law firm.

Effective: 1 May 2026 · Applies to: rentoo.in and connect@rentoo.in · Questions: connect@rentoo.in
01 — What we collect

The minimum we need to reply.

When you message us on WhatsApp, call, email, or use the contact form, we record your name, phone number, and email — whichever you give us. We also note the property IDs you ask about (like #01 or C-3) so we can pull up the file when we reply.

That's it. We don't run forms with thirty fields. We don't try to guess your salary band. We don't ask you to upload anything to reach us.

02 — Nothing required just to inquire

You don't hand over any ID to browse or message us.

To send an inquiry, all we need is your name and a phone number. While you're browsing listings or chatting with us on WhatsApp, we never ask for:

  • Income data, salary slips, or bank statements
  • Social-media profiles or background-check reports
  • Anything uploaded — no documents, no forms with thirty fields

The one exception is verification and the rental agreement. When a deal moves forward, we do require a government ID (Aadhaar, PAN, or passport) and employment ID / proof — for both tenant and owner verification and to draw up the lease. This is collected only at that stage, kept securely, used solely for the agreement, and never shared beyond the parties to it.

03 — How we use what we collect

Reply to you. Send matching listings. That's it.

We use your contact info to reply to your inquiry and, if you've told us what you're looking for, to send matching properties on WhatsApp when they come in.

We don't run drip campaigns. We don't sell newsletters. We don't have a CRM that messages you on your birthday. If something genuinely fits what you asked for, we'll send it — usually a single message, sometimes with a photo. You can tell us to stop any time and we will.

04 — Cookies & analytics

One analytics tool. No ad tracking.

We use Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymisation. It tells us roughly how many people visit, which listings get clicked, and which pages are slow. We don't use that data to follow you across the web.

We don't install third-party advertising cookies. There's no Meta Pixel, no LinkedIn Insight tag, no retargeting. If you see a Rentoo ad somewhere (we run a handful), it's because of where it appears — not because of who you are.

05 — Sharing

Your phone goes to the owner only after you agree to a visit.

The one place we ever share your information is with the owner of a specific property, and only after you've told us you'd like to visit. We share your name and phone so they can coordinate access. We tell you which owner before we do.

We never sell your contact info. We don't share it with other brokers, property portals, or third-party marketers. There's no affiliate network. The list of people who get your phone is: us, and one owner per visit you've agreed to.

06 — Your rights

Ask us to access, correct, or delete your data.

Write to connect@rentoo.in and we'll respond within 7 days. You can ask us to:

  • Send you everything we have on file about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your messages, name, and contact details
  • Stop sending you matching listings (we'll confirm in writing)

We don't ask for a reason. We don't make you fill out a form. If the request is from the phone or email we already have on file, we'll process it on the same business day.

07 — Data retention

Inquiries: 12 months. Signed leases: indefinite.

We keep inquiry messages and contact details for 12 months from your last interaction with us, then we delete them. If you asked about a property in June and haven't messaged since, we'll clear your file the following June.

For tenants and owners who've actually signed a lease through us, we keep the signed agreement and basic transaction record indefinitely. That's required for tax filing, audits, and the rare landlord-tenant dispute that comes up years later. We don't keep anything beyond the signed paper and the rent amount.

08 — Contact

Questions, complaints, or weird edge cases.

The fastest channel is WhatsApp, but for anything privacy-related please email so we have a written record on both sides.
