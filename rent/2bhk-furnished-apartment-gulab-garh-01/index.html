About this home

A bright 2BHK apartment in RR Heights, a five-minute walk from Gulab Garh market and the main Mansarovar arterial road. The flat sits on the third floor of a five-storey building with a working lift and 24-hour security.

Both bedrooms are reasonably sized — the master fits a queen bed with a cupboard, the second works as a study or kids' room. Available for family or working professionals. The owner prefers a one-year lease with a two-month deposit. Pets case-by-case.
