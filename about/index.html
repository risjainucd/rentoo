About · Jaipur, since 2021

A small Jaipur brokerage. Run by people who pick up the phone.

Rentoo is a hand-run rental brokerage. We don't list everything we find — we list what we'd be comfortable putting a friend into. That's the whole product.

Why we started this.

Most rental searches in Jaipur start the same way: a forwarded WhatsApp picture, a phone number that goes to voicemail, three site visits where the photo and the flat don't match, and a brokerage demand at the end that nobody told you about.

We didn't want to fix the whole rental market. We wanted to be the small, accountable thing inside it — visit every property, write our own notes, quote our fee up front, and reply on WhatsApp before the lead goes cold. Five years in, that's still the bar.

How we actually work.

We visit the property.

Every listing is walked through by someone on the team. We photograph what's actually there, not what the owner sent us last year.

We write the listing ourselves.

Short, honest notes about the flat, the building, the neighbours, the catch. If there's no parking, we say so. If the bathroom is small, we say so.

You message us on WhatsApp.

Most inquiries come in by WhatsApp. Median reply time is under 30 minutes during business hours. No bots — actual humans you can call back.

We line up the visit.

We schedule with the owner, coordinate access, and ride along if it's a tricky building. You're not on your own.

Brokerage you'll know up front.

We're a brokerage, and we're straight about it. The fee is agreed before you see the flat — same rate for everyone, no surprise demand at signing.
