About this space

A fitted, lock-and-key office on the second floor of Empire Tower, two minutes from the C-Scheme circle on Mirza Ismail Road. The layout opens with a small reception and waiting area, then a corridor of three private cabins on the south wall, an open workstation floor of 18 desks in the middle, and a conference room and server room at the back. Pantry, two attached washrooms, a small store.

Suitable for consulting firms, legal practices, CA offices, and small tech teams up to 25 people. The building has 24×7 access with a biometric main door and a separate stair entry for late nights. Power load is 25KW with a diesel generator backup — works through Jaipur's usual evening cuts without a hiccup.

Owner prefers a three-year lease with a six-month deposit, but is open to a two-year term for the right tenant. Furniture shown in the cabins stays — chairs and tables on the open floor are negotiable.
