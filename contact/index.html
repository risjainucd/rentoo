How we usually help

Most messages fall in one of three buckets
For tenants

Finding the right place.

Tell us BHK, area, budget, move-in date, and anything else that matters — pets, school proximity, parking. We'll send 3 to 5 hand-verified options with photos and notes, then schedule visits. Our fee is agreed up front, before any visit.

For owners

Listing your property.

We visit the property, take photos, and write the listing ourselves. No upfront fee — we get paid when the place rents. If we don't think we can rent it at your asking, we'll tell you that too, in plain language.

For returning clients

Renewal & second property.

Lease coming up? Looking for a second place — bigger flat, office, weekend home? Just send a one-line message. We keep your file, so we won't ask the same questions twice.
