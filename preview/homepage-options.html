C · Curated + Searchable (Hybrid)

Brand at the top, utility close behind. Editorial hero with the tagline, search panel inside it, "Verified by Rentoo" on every featured listing, trust strip. Best balance for the boutique brokerage position.

Best for: trust + conversion
Weakness: more design surface than B

What shipped: the live homepage took option C as a starting point and then evolved beyond it — into the editorial "Curator's Catalog" direction with sandstone background, monospace catalog IDs (#01, #02), terracotta accent, and a personal byline from Rishabh. See it at the live homepage.
